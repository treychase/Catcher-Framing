Catcher framing in the shadow zone

Every take on the edge of the zone is a coin the umpire flips, and a catcher can weight it. This grades catchers on the only pitches where that happens, the shadow: the band a third of a zone either side of the edge. A ball stolen for a strike earns credit that grows with how far off the plate it was, a strike given away costs more the deeper in the zone it was, and the result is checked against a gradient-boosted called-strike model that knows the location, the movement, the count and who was behind the plate.

Method

Data. Every regular-season pitch from Baseball Savant for the seasons shown, reduced to takes the plate umpire had to call: called strikes, balls and blocked balls, with pitchouts and intentional balls removed. Statcast's own umpire field is empty, so the home plate umpire comes from the MLB Stats API and is joined on the game.

Model. A LightGBM classifier on plate location, the batter's zone top and bottom, horizontal and induced vertical break, the count, batter and pitcher handedness, and the umpire as a native categorical feature, so each umpire can carry his own zone shape and not just a single shift. The catcher is deliberately left out: whatever the model cannot explain about a call is what framing is credited with. Hyperparameters come from a grid search scored on log loss, with folds grouped by game so no game's pitches sit on both sides of a split; diagnostics are on a held-out fifth of games. The probabilities used for framing are out-of-fold, so no pitch is scored by a model that saw it.

Zone. Positions are in zone units: 0 is the middle, ±1 is the edge of the plate widened by a ball's radius horizontally, and the batter's own sz_bot and sz_top widened the same way vertically. Distance from the middle is the larger of the two. The shadow runs from 0.67 to 1.33, which is how Savant draws its attack zones.

Validation. The grade is transparent but blunt: it knows where a pitch was, not who called it or what it looked like on the way in. The model knows both. The check is whether the two rank catchers the same way, whether the extra credit for distance goes to pitches the model also thought unlikely, and whether each measure repeats from one season to the next.
